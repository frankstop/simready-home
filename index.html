SimReady Residence Controls

Built with open-source 3D assets from nvidia/simready-assets (CC-BY-4.0), converted from OpenUSD with PBR materials and SimReady physics metadata.

Walk Mode
W A S D or Arrow keys to walk; hold Shift to move faster; drag canvas (or double-click for Pointer Lock) to look around.
Orbit / Dollhouse
Click Orbit (or press V) to zoom and rotate around the home. Toggle Roof (C) for a full architectural cutaway.
Inspect & Interact
Click any piece of furniture, appliance, or decor to inspect its NVIDIA SimReady dimensions, mass, friction, and semantic labels. Click wooden doors to swing them open or shut.
Lighting & Rooms
Press L to cycle Daylight, Golden Hour, and Warm Evening interior lighting. Press 1–6 or click room tabs to jump between rooms.
